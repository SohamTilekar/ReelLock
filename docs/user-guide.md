# User Setup & Configuration Guide

Follow these steps to set up **ReelLock** on your Android device.

---

## Step 1: Install ReelLock

1. Download the latest `ReelLock-v1.0.0.apk` from the [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/ReelLock/ReelLock/releases) page.
2. Open the downloaded file and confirm installation (allow installation from unknown sources if prompted).

---

## Step 2: Grant System Permissions

Launch **ReelLock** from your launcher. You will see two permission configuration cards:

### 1. Enable Accessibility Service (Required)
1. Tap **Enable Accessibility Service** in the ReelLock app dashboard.
2. Android system settings will open. Navigate to **Installed Apps** or **Downloaded Services**.
3. Select **ReelLock** and switch the toggle to **ON**.
4. Confirm system warning dialogs.

> [!NOTE]
> Accessibility Service permission is essential for ReelLock to detect when Instagram Reels views are launched.

### 2. Grant Overlay Permission (Required for Overlay Mode)
1. If you plan to use **Overlay Mode**, tap **Grant Overlay Permission**.
2. Switch the **Allow display over other apps** toggle to **ON** for ReelLock.

---

## Step 3: Choose Your Blocking Action Mode

In the ReelLock control dashboard, select your preferred blocking behavior:

| Mode | Behavior | Best Used For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ↩️ **Auto Press Back** | Triggers Android back button instantly when a Reel opens, taking you back to your feed or previous screen. | Frictionless feed browsing without Reels. |
| 🏠 **Go to Home Screen** | Instantly sends you to your launcher home screen. | Complete break from phone usage. |
| ⛔ **Blocker Overlay** | Displays a full-screen alert overlay over the Reel preventing video viewing. | Conscious habit breaking & awareness. |

---

## Step 4: Verification

Open Instagram and navigate to the Reels tab or tap a Reel in your feed. ReelLock will immediately intervene according to your chosen mode!
